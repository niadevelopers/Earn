Module 1 · The Foundation

Know What You're Selling — Deeply

Before you walk into a single shop, you need to understand this product better than the people you're selling it to. That's what creates authority — and authority closes deals.

📒
What the App Does
It's an advanced offline bookkeeping app for businesses. Tracks sales, stock, debts, and generates profit reports — without internet. Works on any phone or laptop. One device, one activation code, locked for life.
🏪
Who Needs It
Every shop that sells something: hardware stores, pharmacies, supermarkets, salons, boutiques, kiosks, stationers. Even businesses with a POS system still need it — POS tracks transactions, this tracks profitability.
💡
The Core Insight (Memorise This)
Most business owners know their sales but not their actual profit. They can't tell you this week's net without manual calculations. This app does it automatically — in real time. That's the pain point you press.
📵
Why Offline Matters
Kenya's internet is unreliable, especially in markets and industrial areas. An offline app that still works at 2% battery with no data is not a feature — it's a selling point. Lead with it when you hear "internet problems."
How the Commission Works — Exactly
This is the most important thing to understand before you start. There is no fixed commission. Your commission is whatever you charge above Ksh 500. The admin requires Ksh 500 to release the activation code — you pay that first from the client's cash, and whatever remains is yours, instantly.

📍 Scenario: You've just walked in. Owner or cashier at the counter. They haven't seen you before.
NEPQ PRINCIPLE · CURIOSITY FIRST
Don't mention the product. Don't say "I'm selling something." Open with a question about their situation. Curiosity = rapport. Rapport = listening. Listening = closing.
YOU
"Habari boss. Nilikuwa napita hapa area — naomba dakika moja tu. Unafanya bookkeeping yako vipi hivi sasa hivi — unaandika manually ama una system?"
👉 Don't pitch. Don't say "I have an app." Just ask. Silence after this is good — let them answer.
THEM
"Naandika tu..." / "Nina POS..." / "Accountant wangu anashughulikia..."
YOU
"Interesting. Na kwa sasa — kama ungeniambia net profit yako ya wiki iliyopita bila kuhesabu manually, unaweza?"
👉 Most will say no. That hesitation IS your opening. Don't jump yet — pause, let it land.
YOU
"Exactly. Ukiniambia — ningekuonyesha tool inayofanya hiyo automatically kwa dakika moja — ungekuwa tayari kuona?"
👉 This is the "permission ask." Almost nobody says no to seeing something. You're now invited.
WHY THIS WORKS · LOSS AVERSION
You never said "I'm selling." You asked a question that surfaced their own blind spot. By the time they say "yes, show me," they've already admitted the gap. Psychologically, they're pre-sold. You just need to deliver.
📍 Scenario: They've engaged. You're now 2-3 minutes in. Time to deepen the pain.
NEPQ PRINCIPLE · CONSEQUENCE QUESTIONS
Ask them to quantify their own loss. When someone says a number out loud — even an estimate — they feel it. Loss aversion kicks in. You're not creating a problem, you're surfacing one that already exists.
YOU
"Ukiniambia — roughly, kwa mwezi, kuna difference gani kati ya pesa inayoingia na inayobaki after expenses?"
YOU
"Na je — kuna wakati umewahi shuku kama pesa fulani imetoka bila kujua exactly wapi?"
👉 If they say yes to either — you're in. Their body language will shift. They're remembering a real frustration.
YOU
"Exactly — na hiyo ni common sana kwa businesses zinazofanya vizuri actually. Problem si pesa — ni visibility. Ukijua exact numbers real-time, unaweza control vizuri zaidi."
👉 Reframe the problem as common, not shameful. Now you're a peer, not a salesperson.
YOU
"Sawa — na kama ningekuonyesha jinsi ya kuona hiyo automatically, bila internet, bila accountant — kwa simu yako hii tu — unadhani ingekusaidia?"
KEY PHRASE TO MEMORISE
"Problem si pesa — ni visibility." This line separates you from every other salesperson they've met. It sounds like a business consultant, not a vendor. Use it every time.
📍 Scenario: Permission granted. Ask to use their device. This is where the deal is won.
NEPQ PRINCIPLE · OWNERSHIP PSYCHOLOGY
The moment the app is running on their own device, the psychology shifts. It feels like theirs. Activation is just unlocking what's already there. Never demo on your phone if you can avoid it.
YOU
"Sawa — niambie, niitumie simu yako haraka. Itachukua dakika mbili. [download link]. Inakuja..."
👉 While it downloads, keep talking. Don't let silence create doubt.
YOU
"Unaona hii? Ingiza sale yako yoyote ya leo hapa — weka product, bei. Angalia hapa... inaonyesha net profit automatically. Bila calculator. Bila mtu."
YOU
"Na ukijaribu kufunga data, bado inafanya kazi. Offline kabisa. Hii ni data yako tu — haiko server yoyote. Device yako peke yako."
👉 Address the "data privacy" and "internet" concerns proactively — before they raise them. This is called pre-handling objections.
YOU
"Kama ungekuwa unatumia hii jana asubuhi — unajua ungestop kuuliza accountant na kuona mwenyewe. Unaona kama inafanya sense?"
CRITICAL MOVE
Hand the phone back to them after showing the demo. Let them touch it themselves. Don't hold it. The moment their fingers are on it, the sale is 80% done.
📍 Scenario: Demo done. Interest is clear. Time to close. This is the 30-second moment that matters.
NEPQ PRINCIPLE · ASSUMPTIVE CLOSE
Never ask "would you like to buy it?" Instead assume the yes and ask about the next step. "Shall we activate it?" not "Do you want it?" One triggers momentum. The other triggers analysis.
YOU
"Sawa boss — app iko tayari kwa simu yako. Ni 990 bob tu — one-time, hakuna subscription, hakuna renewal. Tuweke active sasa?"
👉 Start at 990. You can always negotiate down. Never start low.
THEM
"Sawa, tuweke." → CLOSE. Collect payment. Activate immediately.
THEM
"Ni ghali kidogo..." [hesitation]
YOU
"Naelewa. Ukiniambia — unagharamu ngapi kwa accountant au kwa errors kwa mwezi? Hii ni mara moja tu. Sawa tufanye 750?"
👉 Anchor against recurring costs. Then come down to 750 — still Ksh 250 commission for you.
YOU
"Ukishapay, simu yako itakuwa activated in under 2 minutes. Tutaisha haraka — una wateja wanaongoja."
AFTER THEY PAY — EXACT FLOW
1. Collect cash. 2. Note the device code from their phone. 3. Send Ksh 500 to admin via M-Pesa. 4. WhatsApp device code to admin. 5. Receive activation code (usually under 2 min). 6. Enter code. 7. App activates. 8. Your commission stays with you. Done.
📍 Scenario: They said "come back tomorrow" or "let me think." Here's how to close them later without being annoying.
NEPQ PRINCIPLE · THE SOFT ASSUMPTIVE
When following up, assume the deal is still alive. Don't ask "did you think about it?" That triggers defensiveness. Act as if the decision was already made and you're just confirming logistics.
YOU (leaving)
"Sawa — nitakuachia namba yangu. Ukiamua leo — nipigie tu na nitakuactivate remotely bila kurudi. Bei itakuwa hiyo hiyo. Na si lazima unipigie — unaweza nitext."
👉 Low pressure exit. You've given them an easy path to say yes later without awkwardness.
YOU (next day text)
"Boss — ni mimi kutoka jana, nilikuonyesha bookkeeping app. App bado iko simu yako. Uko available leo jioni tuweke active — itachukua dakika 2 tu?"
👉 Short. Friendly. Not pushy. Mentions app is already on their phone — lowers activation friction.
IF NO RESPONSE AFTER 2 DAYS:
Move on. Follow-up once, maximum twice. Three contacts with no response = not a buyer right now. Next door.
THE NUMBERS REALITY
Following up once closes roughly 20-30% of "let me think"s. But the real money is in volume — 20 new prospects a day gives you more closes than perfectly following up 5. Both matter. Prioritise new doors first, follow-up second.
Module 3 · Objection Handling

Every "No" Has a Script

Objections are not rejections — they're questions dressed as resistance. Click each one to see the exact word-for-word response that redirects the conversation without pressure.

YOUR RESPONSE
"That's exactly the situation this was built for, boss. POS tracks your transactions — but does it tell you your net profit for today without waiting? Does it track debts, stock variance, or give you a weekly report you can read in 30 seconds? This runs alongside your POS — fills what it doesn't do. Two minutes on your phone and you'll see exactly what I mean."
Why it works: You've validated their system (no threat), reframed the gap as complementary, and asked a set of rhetorical questions they can't say yes to. The "two minutes" line lowers the commitment needed to keep the conversation going.
YOUR RESPONSE
"No problem at all — the demo costs nothing. Let me just show you how it works first, then you decide if it's worth it for your business. If it doesn't make sense, I'll walk out and you never hear from me again. Fair?"
Why it works: You've removed the financial pressure entirely. "Fair?" is a closing micro-question — almost everyone says yes to it. Once they agree to see the demo, the product sells itself. Money objections at the opener are often just guards — not hard no's.
YOUR RESPONSE
"Good — that means this saves them time and gives you visibility without waiting for a monthly report. You shouldn't need to call your accountant to know if today was profitable. This puts that information directly in your hand, right now. Your accountant still does their work — you just stop being blind between reports. Shall I show you how that looks?"
Why it works: You've positioned the app as empowering the owner, not replacing the accountant. "You stop being blind" is psychologically powerful — no business owner wants to feel dependent or in the dark. You've made them the hero of the story.
YOUR RESPONSE
"Understood — and I respect your time. One thing: the demo takes exactly 90 seconds. If it's not for your business, I'll leave and won't come back next week — saving us both time. But if it is useful, next week you'd have wished we did it today. Can I take 90 seconds right now?"
Why it works: You've turned the "busy" objection into a risk-framing: if they dismiss you now, they may regret it. The 90-second claim is a commitment device — very hard to say no to without seeming unreasonable. And you've removed the obligation to "buy" from the ask.
YOUR RESPONSE
"That's a valid concern — and it's exactly why this is designed offline. Your data never leaves your device. It doesn't sync to any server, it doesn't require a login, it doesn't connect to the internet at all. The only person who can see your business numbers is you, on this specific phone. Here — let me show you: [turn off data]. Still running. See?"
Why it works: The physical demo — turning off data — is the most powerful response to a trust objection. You've replaced words with proof. "I trust what I can see" is human nature. Do this move every time someone mentions security or internet concerns.

Module 5 · Mental Game

The Field Is Won in Your Head First

Your product is good. Your scripts are tested. What separates daily earners from people who quit after a week isn't skill — it's mindset. Internalize these before you step out.

📊
Rejections Are Data, Not Verdicts
Every "no" tells you something — wrong timing, wrong entry, wrong opener. Log it mentally. Adjust. One rejection does not mean the next door will reject you. The average door that becomes a sale took 4–6 preceding rejections to identify the right approach. You're building a dataset, not taking a beating.
⚡
Speed Signals Confidence
A slow, uncertain pitch signals doubt. Doubt is contagious — the client catches it and says no. Know your product so thoroughly that your delivery is relaxed and fast. Authority doesn't come from speaking loudly. It comes from speaking like someone who has done this a hundred times. Practice the opener 20 times before you walk in anywhere.
🔢
Volume Is the Only Strategy at First
Your close rate will start low — maybe 1 in 15. That's normal. The solution is not perfection, it's volume. 20 shops = at least 1 close. 5 blocks = 5 closes potential. Stack your visits. The numbers will teach you faster than any script. After 100 visits, your rate will double without you trying.
🛠️
You're Solving a Real Problem
This isn't a con. Every business owner losing money to invisible leaks genuinely needs what you have. Walk in with that certainty — it shows. People buy from people who believe in what they sell. The moment you half-believe it, they'll feel it before you've finished your first sentence.
🎯
Target Right, Not Just Hard
A wholesale distributor making Ksh 500k/month is a better prospect than a kiosk doing Ksh 5k. Read the business before you pitch. Higher revenue = higher pain around missing numbers = higher willingness to pay Ksh 990. Work smart streets, not just long days.
📱
Always Carry Your Download Link
Your phone is your office. Have the download link pinned in WhatsApp, saved in notes, on your lock screen. The moment there's hesitation about getting the link, you've lost momentum. Be frictionless. Any delay in the demo is a door for doubt to walk in.
